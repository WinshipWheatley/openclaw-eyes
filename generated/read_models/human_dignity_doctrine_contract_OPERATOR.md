# Human Dignity Doctrine Contract

OpenClaw now has a nonsectarian doctrine rail for dignity, common good, human review, fair work, anti-domination, and receipt-backed authority. It records constraints only; no live policy mutation or external action occurred.

## Summary
- Human judgment stays above machine authority.
- Consequential actions require proof, consent, review, and humane next steps.
- The doctrine is nonsectarian and operational; it is not product-surface proselytizing.

## Operational Principles
- Human Dignity
- Common Good
- Subsidiarity
- Social Justice
- Universal Destination Of Goods
- Preferential Protection Of Vulnerable
- Just Work And Fair Wages
- Anti Technocratic Domination
- Human Limits And Care
- Accountable AI Authority

## Blocked Patterns
- People As Metrics Only
- Hidden Surveillance
- Authority Without Appeal
- Automation Without Consent
- Profit Only Optimization
- Concentrated Power Without Accountability
- Labor Erasure
- Vulnerable Party Invisibility
- Fake Morality By Elite Committee
- Transhumanist Dismissal Of Limits

## Required Design Patterns
- Human Review Gate: A visible review state with affected people, risks, and approval requirements.
- Receipt Backed Authority: Source proof refs and receipts before completion or external action labels.
- Local First When Sensitive: Local-first handling and cloud/provider blocks unless explicitly gated.
- Operator Consent: Consent or approval receipt before action.
- Explainable Next Safe Move: Human-readable next safe move without machine sludge.
- Appeal Or Reversal Path: Appeal/reversal field or blocked state if not available.
- Power Concentration Warning: Warning when a few parties capture control or benefit.
- Fair Access And Portability: Portability or fair-access consideration without exposing private data.
- Vulnerable Party Check: Affected vulnerable-party field and risk disposition.
- Labor Impact Check: Labor impact summary and human review requirement.

## Examples
- Automation: Dignity check required; consent, proof, and appeal/reversal path required; hidden automation blocked.
- Labor: Labor impact check required; fair work and just wage concerns surfaced; not automatically approved.
- Private data: Local-first and consent/protection gates required; raw-body extraction blocked by default.
- Pricing/access: Fair access, portability, and broad benefit must be considered; not only elite-user optimization.
- Capital Hilton: Financial action requires proof, consent/approval, no hidden send or submit, and human relationship context preserved with the contact.

## Authority
- No live policy mutation.
- No workflow run.
- No agent dispatch.
- No external action.
- No surveillance, pricing, or labor decision.
- No credential handling or raw-body ingestion.
