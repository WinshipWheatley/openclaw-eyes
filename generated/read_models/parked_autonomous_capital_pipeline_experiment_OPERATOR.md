# Parked Autonomous Capital Pipeline R&D Experiment v0

## ELIWINSHIP Summary

This preserves a high-risk future thought experiment: compare pure machine execution against a hybrid version where human judgment can change macro-risk, ethics, and capital direction. It is interesting as a way to study human utility against automation, not as an active business plan. It is parked.

## Architecture Preserved

- `zero_human_control_experiment`: Pure machine execution with a dual-agent verification concept. Status: `BLOCKED_CONCEPT_ONLY`.
- `hybrid_human_variable_experiment`: Machine execution plus a human exception gate for macro-risk, ethical, and capital-direction guidance. Status: `BLOCKED_CONCEPT_ONLY`.
- `meta_ceo_differential_layer`: Compare machine-only and hybrid outcomes across adaptability, efficiency, and human-utility delta. Status: `CONCEPTUAL_ONLY`.
- `business_3_sovereign_shell_capstone`: Long-horizon synthesis concept for macro-scale problem solving. Status: `SPECULATIVE_PARKED`.

## Five Parked Phases

- `zero_infrastructure_bootstrapping`: `BLOCKED_FUTURE_GATED`. Blocked actions: spending, domain purchase, ad purchase, deployment, checkout creation, account creation.
- `micro_saas_self_hosted_compute`: `BLOCKED_FUTURE_GATED`. Blocked actions: cloud accounts, API keys, CI/CD, paid compute, rollback automation.
- `portfolio_diversification_automated_acquisition`: `BLOCKED_FUTURE_GATED`. Blocked actions: brokerage crawling, acquisitions, contracts, payments, external account access.
- `autonomous_treasury_dividend_routing`: `BLOCKED_FUTURE_GATED`. Blocked actions: payouts, crypto addresses, banking, yield loops, outbound financial transfers.
- `business_3_sovereign_shell`: `SPECULATIVE_NOT_ACTIONABLE`. Blocked actions: implementation, capital movement, external operations, runtime execution.

## Why High Risk

- It touches future spending, accounts, marketplace terms, deployment, payments, payouts, contracts, tax, legal/compliance, and autonomous execution boundaries.
- Those are exactly the areas OpenClaw must not improvise.
- No part of this read-model implies this will make money or is ready to run.
