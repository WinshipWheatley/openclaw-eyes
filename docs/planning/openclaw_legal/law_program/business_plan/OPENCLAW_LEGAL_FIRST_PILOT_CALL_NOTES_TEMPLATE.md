# OpenClaw Legal First Pilot Call Notes Template

## Purpose

This is an internal, sanitized notes template for an exploratory first conversation with a possible OpenClaw Legal pilot firm.

It is not buyer-facing sales copy, not a final offer, not a contract, not legal advice, and not emergency support. It is only for recording workflow-fit signals and deciding the next safe internal step.

## Sanitization rule

Do not include confidential matter facts, client names, privileged material, legal advice content, private paths, actual filenames, raw document text, party names, witness names, case numbers, court deadlines tied to identifiable matters, or facts that would identify a client or matter.

Write generalized workflow notes only. If the firm starts giving matter-specific details, stop collecting detail and redirect to the workflow category.

End with notes, not commitments. Use the first-pilot go/no-go review packet before scheduling anything beyond synthetic/public demo info.
