# OpenClaw Legal First Pilot Outreach Readiness Summary

## Purpose

This is a short internal operator-facing summary for one question:

```text
Are we ready to talk to a potential first pilot firm, and under what exact limits?
```

It is not buyer-facing sales copy, not a final offer, not a contract, not legal advice, and not production approval.

## Current verdict

- Synthetic/public demo: Go.
- Paid pilot discussion: Conditional.
- Real deployment: No-Go until separately proven.
- `Run Synthetic Dry Run` is allowed only under the synthetic demo validation package, fixed synthetic values, and sanitized status-only output.
- Real-matter GUI Run, Reset, arbitrary bridge execution, real matter through the app, Connect, queue/ETA, OCR/model distribution, unsupported-file feature expansion, cloud/external model processing, and broader Run/Reset behavior remain gated or NO-GO.

The answer is: yes, a first conversation is allowed if it stays exploratory, bounded, and honest. It may discuss a synthetic/public demo and conditional paid-pilot planning. It must not imply real deployment readiness.

## What can be discussed safely

Safe outreach can discuss:

- the general problem: small-firm discovery files become scattered, unclear, and hard to track
- private local discovery infrastructure as the product category
- a narrow first pilot shape: one firm, one scoped workflow, one primary machine, one local vault, agreed file types only
- synthetic/public demo availability
- supported-file workflow concepts
- source tracking, hashing, local extraction, local search, reports, review packets, sanitized support packets, and Alternative Methods where accurately described as built, prototype-only, or roadmap-only
- the need for written scope, support, hardware, data, backup, offboarding, attorney-review, and payment terms before any paid pilot

Use calm operational language. Keep every capability tied to current proof or clearly labeled future work.

## What must not be promised

Do not promise:

- legal advice
- legal conclusions
- privilege calls
- attorney replacement
- emergency/court-deadline support
- full e-discovery replacement
- support for every file type
- raw matter remote review
- unlimited support
- broad custom development
- broader Run/Reset GUI behavior
- arbitrary bridge execution or Real-matter GUI Run from the app
- real matter mode
- Connect, queue/ETA, or model distribution
- timeline, contradiction, privilege-screening, email/cloud connector, or broad OCR/media workflows
- production deployment readiness
- guaranteed completeness, search coverage, privilege outcome, or legal outcome

Any exception must be written, narrow, paid if appropriate, and separately approved before work starts.

## Minimum pre-outreach checklist

Before talking to a potential first pilot firm, confirm:

- [ ] Current Legal checkpoint is known.
- [ ] Synthetic/public demo validation package exists.
- [ ] First-pilot scope is current.
- [ ] Buyer brief is current and reviewed for overpromising.
- [ ] Pilot readiness checklist is current.
- [ ] Pilot terms outline is current.
- [ ] First-pilot go/no-go review packet is current.
- [ ] Support boundary is understood.
- [ ] No real matter data is needed for the conversation.
- [ ] No private matter data will enter prompts, docs, fixtures, support packets, or non-local tools.
- [ ] File-type limits are ready to explain plainly.
- [ ] Emergency support exclusions are ready to explain plainly.
- [ ] Attorney-review and not legal advice boundaries are ready to explain plainly.

If any item is missing, pause outreach and fix the internal packet first.

## Red flags that stop outreach

Stop outreach if the buyer or workflow needs:

- emergency litigation help
- legal conclusions or privilege decisions
- unsupported critical file types immediately
- broad remote access to raw matter data
- full e-discovery replacement
- unlimited support or open-ended custom work
- Run/Reset GUI behavior as a condition of pilot
- Connect, queue/ETA, or model distribution now
- cloud/non-local matter processing by default
- real deployment before written support, liability, data, hardware, backup, and offboarding terms exist

Do not turn a red flag into a quick feature promise.

## Exact next allowed outreach posture

Allowed posture:

```text
OpenClaw Legal may be discussed as a promising local-first discovery workflow for a narrow first pilot, subject to written scope, support, data, hardware, backup, offboarding, attorney-review, and payment terms.
```

Do not present it as a launched product, general release, production deployment, legal advice tool, litigation support service, or full e-discovery platform.

## Synthetic/public demo

A synthetic/public demo can be shown if it uses only synthetic or public-safe data and follows the synthetic demo validation package.

The demo may use `Run Synthetic Dry Run` only under the synthetic demo validation package, fixed synthetic fixture values, and sanitized status-only output. It must not use real matter data, inspect private matter paths, execute arbitrary bridge behavior, enable Reset, enable Real-matter GUI Run, or imply production readiness.

## Paid pilot discussion

Paid pilot discussion is allowed only as conditional planning.

The first paid pilot cannot begin until written terms cover scope, payment, support, hardware, data ownership, backups, retention, offboarding, liability, no-legal-advice language, attorney-review requirement, emergency-support exclusions or pricing, unsupported-file policy, privacy, and termination/pause rights.

The correct next step after buyer interest is a planning session, not implementation.

## Why real deployment remains No-Go

Real deployment remains No-Go because real-matter operation requires separately written and proven terms for support, liability, data handling, hardware ownership, backups, retention, offboarding, privacy, attorney review, and emergency-support boundaries.

The current package supports outreach, synthetic/public demo, and conditional paid-pilot planning. It does not prove real deployment readiness.

## Next-step rule

If outreach produces interest, use the first-pilot go/no-go review packet before scheduling anything beyond a synthetic/public demo.

If the review packet produces Conditional Go, schedule a planning session and draft terms. Do not implement features.

If the review packet produces No-Go, pause. Do not build custom features to rescue the opportunity.
