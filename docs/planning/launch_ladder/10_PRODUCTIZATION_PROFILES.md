## Profile Rule

Productization means selectable modules with explicit authority, data boundaries, proof, deployment assumptions, and validation. It does not mean copying Winship's personal stack wholesale.

The Multi-OpenClaw Command Atlas is the long-range product horizon for seeing those profiles together. It must support personal OpenClaw and future client/company deployments as separate governed systems, then let the operator zoom into departments, agents/systems/subsystems/modules, launch goals, Launch Ladders, steps, evidence artifacts, and exact docs/code/prompts/validation.

For every profile and zoom level, the atlas should answer what the thing is, what it can do, its North Star, readiness, blockers, evidence, and next safe Launch Ladder. The atlas remains a map and evidence browser, not authority.

## Personal OpenClaw

Purpose: Winship's local-first operator substrate.

Recommended modules:

- Core runtime law and architecture principles.
- Modular readiness ledger.
- Service-control SE kernel.
- MCP progressive discovery.
- Model routing/fallback policy.
- Cassandra with broker-gated Google surfaces.
- Guardian approvals.
- Hermes advisory packet contract.
- Dashboard/operator reporting and Launch Ladder console.
- Source-set/ChatGPT ingest workflow.

Do not include broad autonomy, live service mutation, external protected packets, or unified memory claims until proven.

## Local-First Law Firm Discovery

Purpose: private local discovery and matter workflow.

Recommended modules:

- Core runtime law adapted for firm/operator context.
- Legal/local discovery product with private vault contract.
- Local model privacy boundary with no-cloud default for real matters.
- Guardian approval for exports/support packets.
- Service-control SE kernel only if local services are installed.
- Source-set workflow only after sanitizer and customer approval.

Do not include Cassandra Gmail behavior, external expert escalation with raw matter data, broad MCP roots, or cloud fallback by default.

## Creative/Business Assistant

Purpose: operator assistant for scheduling, light inbox/workflow support, project tracking, and safe drafting.

Recommended modules:

- Cassandra-like assistant with explicit capability registry.
- Google broker with actor/capability/class table.
- Guardian approval for external sends and irreversible actions.
- Dashboard/operator reporting.
- Local model privacy boundary for drafts/summaries.
- Launch Ladder route model for next safe action.

Do not include bank/payment direct verification, inbox roaming, autonomous follow-up, Telegram/SMS sends, or Gmail body analysis without explicit policy and tests.

## Company Internal Advisory Assistant

Purpose: non-canonical internal consultant over approved company docs/source sets.

Recommended modules:

- Core runtime law adapted for company policy.
- Hermes-like packet-in/proposal-out advisory consultant.
- MCP progressive discovery with docs-only default.
- Expert escalation through sanitized packet and approval receipt.
- Source-set/ingest workflow with withheld-surface manifests.
- Read-only dashboard/operator reporting.

Do not let advisory outputs become canonical decisions. Company systems of record remain authoritative unless a brokered integration is built and approved.

## Service-Control SE Kernel Package

Purpose: safe install/ownership/readiness planning for any deployment with local services.

Required pieces:

- Static service inventory.
- Systemd/manual/legacy ownership table.
- Forbidden control paths.
- Dry-run/default refusal rules.
- Live-state verification plan, separately approved.
- Validation map entries.

Do not run service checks, installers, or unit mutations as part of product discovery.

## Productization Readiness Checklist

Before any deployment profile becomes a product candidate, it needs:

- Customer-specific data taxonomy.
- Authority matrix.
- Withheld-surface list.
- Source-set manifest.
- Validation map.
- Approval/broker plan.
- Freshness/stale rules.
- Installation/runbook boundary.
- Rollback/decommission path.
- Atlas placement: deployment, department, modules, launch goals, ladders, evidence, and next safe action.
