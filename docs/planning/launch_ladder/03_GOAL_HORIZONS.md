## Horizon Card Shape

Each goal card should include:

- `horizon`: short, medium, or long.
- `goal`: concrete outcome.
- `current_status`: concept, static contract, read-only proof, dry-run proof, bounded action, production candidate, or frozen.
- `evidence_refs`: docs/tests/commits/artifacts/checks.
- `next_safe_slice`: one bounded next action.
- `deferred_work`: preserved follow-up work.
- `stale_conditions`: when to refresh the card.
- `authority_note`: what this card does not authorize.
