# 04 Anti-Drift Rules

## Stop the Slop
- **Big Bounded Strides**: When the road is clear (Rail Map), take substantial moves. Do not force the operator to manage tiny micro-prompts for visible work.
- **Implementation > Doctrine**: If the doctrine is settled in a Packet Rail, stop writing more doctrine. Implement the proof.

## No Fake Progress
- **No UI Before Grounding**: Do not build dashboards or UI spikes before the underlying data contract and evidence bridge are grounded.
- **No Live Surface Without Gates**: Do not enable runtime, MCP, or external sends before the Action Covenant and security gates are implemented and verified.
- **No Stale Steering**: Generated watch files (e.g., `Operator Watch.md`) are transient windows. They do not steer work. The `Active Handoff` and `Rail Map` are the track.

## Authority Discipline
- **Research != Canonical**: Research docs in `Docs/Planning/` are future source material. They do not authorize current implementation unless promoted to a Packet Rail.
- **Intent != Authority**: Natural language requests for "launch" or "handle it" are intent, not permission. Route them through the Action Covenant gate.

## The Drift Test
If you feel lost or the work feels "floaty," return to the **Operator Loop altitude**:
1. What is the intent?
2. What is the evidence?
3. What is the covenant?
4. What is the visible frame?
